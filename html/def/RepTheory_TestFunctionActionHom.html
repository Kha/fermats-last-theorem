definition module

Test-function (Hecke) action on a smooth complex representation

Throughout, G is a group with a topology and a measurable structure, V a complex vector space, and \pi : G \to \operatorname{End}_{\mathbb C}(V) a monoid homomorphism. ModuleAction.ofHom turns such a \pi into a DistribMulAction of G on V, in which g acts as the endomorphism \pi(g); ModuleAction.ofHom_smul records that g \bullet v = \pi(g)v and ModuleAction.smulCommClass_ofHom that this action commutes with the complex scalars, \mathbb{C}-linearity of each \pi(g). Correspondingly, TestFunctionAction.stabilizer_ofHom_eq identifies the stabiliser of v for this action with repStabilizer π v, the subgroup \{g : \pi(g)v = v\}, so that the project's smoothness predicate IsSmoothRep π (openness of every such stabiliser) is equivalent to the statement that every v \in V has open stabiliser for the action (isSmoothRep_iff_forall_hasOpenStabilizer, with the one-directional form hasOpenStabilizer_of_isSmoothRep).

The main construction, heckeSmulHom, takes a smooth \pi and a measure \mu on G that is finite on compact sets, and produces a \mathbb{C}-linear map from SchwartzBruhatSpace.testSpace G — the submodule of locally constant, compactly supported functions G \to \mathbb{C} — to \operatorname{End}_{\mathbb C}(V). A test function \varphi is sent to the endomorphism v \mapsto heckeSmul μ φ v, the finite-range integral of g \mapsto \varphi(g)\,\pi(g)v against \mu: the integrand has finite range and open, hence measurable, fibres, and the integral is the finite sum \sum_w \mu(\text{fibre over } w)\, w with measures read in \mathbb{C} via \mathbb{R}. Additivity and \mathbb{C}-homogeneity in both \varphi and v make this bilinear. heckeSmulHom_apply states the defining formula.

Two groups of auxiliary statements are proved: for a Haar measure \mu on G, an open subgroup has nonzero measure and a compact subgroup has finite measure; and a constant multiple of the indicator function of a subgroup K is invariant under left and right translation by elements of K.

Relation to Mathlib

The action attached to \pi is Mathlib's DistribMulAction.compHom under a local name. The smoothness predicate, the Schwartz–Bruhat test space and the finite-range integral used to define the test-function action are the project's own: the integral is a finite weighted sum over the fibres of a locally constant compactly supported integrand, so no topology or Bochner integration on V is required.

Where it is used

This supplies the module structure of the Hecke algebra of locally constant compactly supported functions on a smooth complex representation, part of the representation-theoretic input for the Langlands–Tunnell theorem used to establish modularity of the residual representation.

    letI := ModuleAction.ofHom π
    MulAction.stabilizer G v = repStabilizer π v := by
  letI := ModuleAction.ofHom π
  ext g

theorem isSmoothRep_iff_forall_hasOpenStabilizer :
    letI := ModuleAction.ofHom π
    IsSmoothRep π ↔ ∀ v : V, HasOpenStabilizer G v := by
  letI := ModuleAction.ofHom π
  refine forall_congr' fun v => ?_
  show IsOpen _ ↔ IsOpen _
  rw [stabilizer_ofHom_eq]

    letI := ModuleAction.ofHom π
    HasOpenStabilizer G v :=
  (isSmoothRep_iff_forall_hasOpenStabilizer π).mp hsm v

variable {G : Type} [Group G] [TopologicalSpace G] [ContinuousMul G] [MeasurableSpace G]
  [OpensMeasurableSpace G]
variable {V : Type} [AddCommGroup V] [Module ℂ V]

noncomputable def heckeSmulHom (π : G →* Module.End ℂ V) (hsm : IsSmoothRep π) (μ : Measure G)
    [IsFiniteMeasureOnCompacts μ] : SchwartzBruhatSpace.testSpace G →ₗ[ℂ] Module.End ℂ V :=
  letI := ModuleAction.ofHom π
  haveI := ModuleAction.smulCommClass_ofHom π
  have hsm' : ∀ v : V, HasOpenStabilizer G v := hasOpenStabilizer_of_isSmoothRep π hsm
  { toFun := fun φ =>
      { toFun := fun v => heckeSmul μ (φ : G → ℂ) v
        map_add' := fun v w => heckeSmul_add_right μ φ.2.1 φ.2.2 (hsm' v) (hsm' w)
        map_smul' := fun c v => heckeSmul_smul_right μ φ.2.1 φ.2.2 (hsm' v) c }
    map_add' := fun φ ψ => by
      ext v
      exact heckeSmul_add_left μ φ.2.1 φ.2.2 ψ.2.1 ψ.2.2 (hsm' v)
    map_smul' := fun c φ => by
      ext v
      exact heckeSmul_smul_left μ φ.2.1 φ.2.2 (hsm' v) c }

theorem heckeSmulHom_apply (π : G →* Module.End ℂ V) (hsm : IsSmoothRep π) (μ : Measure G)
    [IsFiniteMeasureOnCompacts μ] (φ : SchwartzBruhatSpace.testSpace G) (v : V) :
    heckeSmulHom π hsm μ φ v =
      (letI := ModuleAction.ofHom π; heckeSmul μ (φ : G → ℂ) v) :=
