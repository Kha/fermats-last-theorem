Hopf algebra structure on the schematic closure

Throughout, R is a commutative ring, F a field that is an R-algebra, G a commutative ring, and B a subalgebra of F \otimes_R G; for all the substantive statements R is in addition a Noetherian principal ideal domain with F its fraction field, and G is finite and torsion-free as an R-module. Recall that flatClosure B is the R-subalgebra \{g \in G : 1 \otimes g \in B\} of G and that tensorInclusion B is the map \mathrm{flatClosure}\,B \otimes_R \mathrm{flatClosure}\,B \to G \otimes_R G induced by the inclusion. Two triple analogues are defined: tensorInclusion3 B sends x \otimes w \mapsto x \otimes \mathrm{tensorInclusion}\,B\,w into G \otimes_R (G \otimes_R G), and tensorInclusion3' B sends w \otimes z \mapsto \mathrm{tensorInclusion}\,B\,w \otimes z into (G \otimes_R G) \otimes_R G. Since G and the closure are free over the principal ideal domain R, tensorInclusion3 B is injective; further lemmas record that these maps commute with the associators, that tensorInclusion B is multiplicative and unital, and that it intertwines the multiplication maps, the counit, the comultiplication closureComul and the antipode closureAntipode.

From these compatibilities the coalgebra axioms for \mathrm{flatClosure}\,B are verified: coassociativity of closureComul, the two counit laws, multiplicativity and unitality of closureCounit and of closureComul, and the two antipode identities. They are assembled into closureCoalgebra R F hB, closureBialgebra R F hB (given a comultiplication-stability hypothesis hB : IsComulStable R F B, i.e. \Delta carries the closure into the F-span of B \otimes_F B inside the generic fibre) and closureHopfAlgebra R F hB hB' (adding antipode stability, i.e. the antipode of G maps the closure into itself); these are definitions, not registered global instances. Finally, closureIsCocomm states that the closure is cocommutative when G is, closureValBialgHom exhibits the inclusion \mathrm{flatClosure}\,B \to G as a morphism of R-bialgebras whose underlying algebra map is the coercion, and convMul_comp_closureVal states that restricting L-valued points along that inclusion, for a commutative R-algebra L, is multiplicative for the convolution product on algebra homomorphisms.

Relation to Mathlib

The Coalgebra, Bialgebra and HopfAlgebra classes, the bialgebra-homomorphism notation →ₐc[R], and the convolution monoid WithConv on algebra homomorphisms out of a bialgebra are Mathlib's; what is constructed here are such structures on the schematic closure flatClosure B, supplied as definitions rather than as instances.

Where it is used

These structures make the schematic closure of a comultiplication-stable subalgebra of the generic fibre into a finite flat commutative Hopf algebra over the base, so that later modules may speak of the group law, powers and identity on its points. This is the algebraic form of the statement that the schematic closure of a subgroup of the generic fibre of a finite flat commutative group scheme over a principal base is again finite flat, used in the analysis of the finite flat group schemes attached to the p-torsion of the relevant elliptic curves.

theorem tensorInclusion3_assoc_apply {B : Subalgebra F (F ⊗[R] G)}
    (w : ((flatClosure B) ⊗[R] (flatClosure B)) ⊗[R] (flatClosure B)) :
    tensorInclusion3 B
        (TensorProduct.assoc R (flatClosure B) (flatClosure B) (flatClosure B) w)
      = TensorProduct.assoc R G G G (tensorInclusion3' B w) := by
  induction w with
  | zero => simp

      | zero => simp
      | tmul x y => simp

          simp only [TensorProduct.add_tmul, map_add, h₁, h₂]
  | add w₁ w₂ h₁ h₂ => simp [map_add, h₁, h₂]
