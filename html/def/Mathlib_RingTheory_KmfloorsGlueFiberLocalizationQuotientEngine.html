Fibre ring localised at a prime versus Bq​/pBq​

Throughout, B is an algebra over a commutative ring A, \mathfrak p is a prime of A, \mathfrak q is a prime of B lying over \mathfrak p (the LiesOver hypothesis, i.e. \mathfrak p is the contraction of \mathfrak q), Q is a prime of the fibre ring \mathfrak p.\mathrm{Fiber}\,B = \kappa(\mathfrak p)\otimes_A B, and B_\mathfrak{q} is any ring Bq realised as the localisation of B at \mathfrak q. The target ring is always Bq modulo the ideal obtained by pushing \mathfrak pB forward along B\to Bq, i.e. B_\mathfrak{q}/\mathfrak pB_\mathfrak{q}.

The comparison map is assembled in three steps. kmfloorsGlue_quotLeg is the ring homomorphism A/\mathfrak p\to B_\mathfrak{q}/\mathfrak pB_\mathfrak{q} induced by A\to B\to B_\mathfrak{q}, legitimate because \mathfrak p dies in the quotient. kmfloorsGlue_quotLeg_units records that it sends non-zero-divisors of A/\mathfrak p to units (here the lying-over hypothesis is used), so it extends by kmfloorsGlue_residueLeg to the residue field \kappa(\mathfrak p), the total quotient ring of A/\mathfrak p. Together with B\to B_\mathfrak{q}/\mathfrak pB_\mathfrak{q} this yields kmfloorsGlue_fiberEval, a ring homomorphism \kappa(\mathfrak p)\otimes_A B\to B_\mathfrak{q}/\mathfrak pB_\mathfrak{q} with x\otimes b\mapsto \mathrm{residueLeg}(x)\cdot\overline b. Under the hypothesis that Q contracts to \mathfrak q along b\mapsto 1\otimes b, kmfloorsGlue_isLocalization_fiberEval asserts that this homomorphism exhibits B_\mathfrak{q}/\mathfrak pB_\mathfrak{q} as a localisation of the fibre ring at the complement of Q; kmfloorsGlue_fiberLocalizationAtPrimeQuotientEquiv converts this into a ring isomorphism (\kappa(\mathfrak p)\otimes_AB)_Q\cong B_\mathfrak{q}/\mathfrak pB_\mathfrak{q}, and kmfloorsGlue_isRegularLocalRing_localizationQuotient_iff transports regularity across it in both directions.

Supporting lemmas: \mathfrak p maps to 0 in the fibre, 1\otimes w=0 for w\in\mathfrak pB, elements outside \mathfrak p become units in the fibre, and b\in\mathfrak q\iff 1\otimes b\in Q. kmfloorsGlue_maximalIdeal_map_eq states that for A_\mathfrak{p} and any ring map A_\mathfrak{p}\to B_\mathfrak{q} compatible with the maps from A, the image ideal of the maximal ideal of A_\mathfrak{p} is exactly \mathfrak pB_\mathfrak{q}. Finally, test cases: for B=A the fibre ring is a domain isomorphic to \kappa(\mathfrak p), its zero ideal contracts to \mathfrak p, and the isomorphism specialises to \kappa(\mathfrak p)\cong A_\mathfrak p/\mathfrak pA_\mathfrak p, instantiated at (\mathbb Z,(3)); a contrasting computation shows that extending (3) to the localisation of \mathbb Z at (0) gives the unit ideal.

Relation to Mathlib

The fibre ring \kappa(\mathfrak p)\otimes_A B and the residue field \kappa(\mathfrak p) are Mathlib's Ideal.Fiber and Ideal.ResidueField; the comparison homomorphism to B_\mathfrak{q}/\mathfrak pB_\mathfrak{q}, the localisation property and the resulting isomorphism are the project's own.

Where it is used

These identifications are used in the commutative-algebra layer to compute local rings of fibres of an algebra map as quotients of localisations, and in particular to transfer regularity of such local rings.
