Hypothesis frame for rigid-level descent at supersingular points

This module defines a single Prop-valued structure, ModularCurve.FullLevel.RigidDescentHyps, which packages as fields the entire hypothesis list of the rigid-level descent statements, so that those statements may bind one term instead of repeating the list. Its parameters are the data the hypotheses speak about: a prime q, a level M', a valuation subring A of \overline{\mathbb Q}, a finite set W of places of \mathrm{modularFunctionFieldC} at level M' over the residue field of A together with a chosen s\in W, an inclusion hle of the base-changed level-M' function field into \mathrm{fieldBar}\,q\,M' and a ConstantReduction R_0 along it; a subfield k_0\subseteq\overline{\mathbb Q} with an element \pi_0 lying in A, auxiliary integers \ell,\ell' with roots of unity \zeta_0,\xi and elements \varpi_t,\varpi_t',\varpi_b, a presentation (K_b,A_b), the intermediate field K_\ell of the Laurent series over k_0 with a distinguished j_\ell\neq0, a maximal-ideal candidate y in the two-chart algebra \mathrm{chartAlgFin} over A_0:=A\cap k_0, a subalgebra B_t inside a valuation subring W_t, the cyclic data (n,\gamma_0,\tau_0,m,\zeta_c), an element a_0 and a finite set ends of subrings of K_\ell.

The fields assert: q\ge5, q\nmid M', that q is a nonunit in A, that W is exactly the set of supersingular places (rational, affine geometric, with supersingular j-value), and that R_0 computes coefficientwise reduction of Laurent series; that A_0 is a henselian discrete valuation ring with uniformiser \pi_0 and algebraically closed residue field, with k_0\cap A a set of representatives mod \mathfrak m_A; primality and coprimality of \ell,\ell', primitivity of \zeta_0,\xi, the relation \varpi_t^{q^2-1}=q\cdot(\text{unit}), K_b=\bot, tameness of \ell'(\ell'^2-1) in A_0; that K_\ell is the k_0-base change of the X_H function field at level (q\ell')^2M' with H=\mathrm{levelH}(q\ell')M' and that j_\ell is the j q-expansion; that y is maximal, contains \pi_0, has supersingular chart coordinate in every characteristic-q algebraically closed residue quotient, lies over s in the sense made precise by the clause hover (agreement of reductions of level-M' functions regular where j is), and that every other such maximal ideal is carried to y by a level automorphism \tau implementing some \gamma^{-1} with \gamma\in\Gamma(q)\cap\Gamma_0(M') in the sense of IsLevelAutAt (matching of q-expansions against \Gamma_H-modular forms slashed by the conjugate matrix \mathrm{conjElemN}). The grouped clauses hS1, hR7, hR9, hR10 record, respectively: the rigid chart (chart algebra inside B_t, B_t formally smooth and finitely presented over A_0 with fraction field K_\ell and special fibre of Krull dimension \le1, W_t a discrete valuation ring cutting out y, a surjection of B_t onto the Drinfeld coordinate ring over the residue field of A_0 with kernel \mathfrak m_{W_t}, equivariant for hSubgroup/hAction under level automorphisms, together with orbit and divisibility clauses for primes containing \pi_0); the cyclic decomposition and the q+1 ends (transitively permuted, each \tau_0-stable, local noetherian, with \mathfrak m-adic completion isomorphic to the crossing model \mathrm{MvPowerSeries}(\mathrm{Fin}\,2)/(X_0X_1-\pi_0^m) in which \tau_0 acts by U\mapsto\zeta_cU, V\mapsto\zeta_c^{-1}V modulo \mathfrak m^2, the Hasse germ j_{q\ell'}-a_0 being a unit times V^e with e\ge1, plus a second valuation subring W_x separating the branches and a finite-type B_x generating O); and two pole/separation clauses producing elements of B_t outside prescribed valuation subrings. The last field is the width identity n=2\,\mathrm{placeWidthChar}\,q\,M'\,s. Several hypotheses needed to phrase later fields (hπ, hAb, hBW, hjK, hle) are parameters rather than fields.

Relation to Mathlib

Mathlib supplies the ambient notions used here (valuation subrings, discrete valuation and henselian local rings, adic completions, formal smoothness, finite presentation, Krull dimension bounds, multivariate power series, congruence subgroups of \mathrm{SL}(2,\mathbb Z), modular forms and q-expansions); the modular-curve function fields, constant reductions, two-chart integral models, Drinfeld coordinate rings, crossing models and level automorphisms combined in this structure are the project's own.

Where it is used

The structure is the common hypothesis frame for the statements that produce, at a supersingular place, a discrete valuation ring and affine chart of an integral model of the full-level modular curve, and the common-chart/branch statements describing its q+1 Igusa-type branches crossing in the UV-model. These feed the descent from the auxiliary rigid level q\ell' down to level q\,M' used in the level-lowering part of the argument.
