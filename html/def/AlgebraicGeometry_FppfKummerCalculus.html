fppf cohomology dimension shifting and Kummer values over Spec ℤ

The first namespace, FppfCohomologyLES, collects the elementary consequences of the long exact cohomology sequence in a sheaf category. For a category C with a Grothendieck topology J (sheafification and Ext-groups for J-sheaves of abelian groups being available) and a short exact sequence S : X_1 \to X_2 \to X_3 of sheaves, with n_1 = n_0 + 1: the connecting map cohomologyδ \delta : H^{n_0}(X_3) \to H^{n_1}(X_1) is injective when H^{n_0}(X_2) is a subsingleton, surjective when H^{n_1}(X_2) is, and bijective when both are, giving the additive equivalence cohomologyDimensionShiftEquiv H^{n_0}(X_3) \simeq H^{n_1}(X_1) and the resulting equality of Nat.cards (with one-sided inequalities under a single vanishing hypothesis and a finiteness instance, Nat.card being 0 on infinite types). Exactness is also recorded as the identities \ker \delta = \operatorname{im} H^{n_0}(g) and \ker H^{n_1}(f) = \operatorname{im}\delta, whence cohomologyCokerEquiv, an equivalence H^{n_0}(X_3)/\operatorname{im} H^{n_0}(g) \simeq H^{n_1}(X_1), and cohomologyKerEquiv, an equivalence H^{n_0}(X_3) \simeq \ker H^{n_1}(f); a zero sheaf has all H^n of cardinality 1, and subsingleton-ness transfers along the dimension shift. Consistency statements for the split sequence attached to the biproduct of the zero sheaf with itself assert that there \delta is bijective and identically zero. These are then restated verbatim for the large fppf site on schemes, and applied to the Kummer sequence for a nonzero exponent p: under the hypothesis that H^1 of the universe-lifted \mathbb{G}_m-sheaf is a subsingleton, \delta onto H^1 of the \mu_p-sheaf is surjective, H^0(\mathbb{G}_m) modulo the image of the p-th power endomorphism gmPowSelf is isomorphic to H^1(\mu_p), the corresponding cardinalities agree, and vanishing of H^{n_0}, H^{n_1} for \mathbb{G}_m forces H^{n_1}(\mu_p) to be trivial.

The second namespace, FppfBigSiteH0Gm, turns the identification of H^0 with global sections into a computation. Naturality of the additive equivalence H^0(F) \simeq F(T), for T terminal, in a morphism of sheaves is proved both for a general site and for the fppf site, and specialised at \operatorname{Spec}\mathbb{Z}, terminal in schemes, to bigSiteH0SectionsAddEquiv H^0(F) \simeq F(\operatorname{Spec}\mathbb{Z}). Combining this with the identification of the sections of the lifted \mathbb{G}_m-sheaf and with gammaSpecZUnitsMulEquiv, the multiplicative equivalence \Gamma(\operatorname{Spec}\mathbb{Z}, \mathcal{O})^\times \simeq \mathbb{Z}^\times, yields gmH0AddEquivIntUnits : H^0_{\mathrm{fppf}}(\operatorname{Spec}\mathbb{Z}, \mathbb{G}_m) \simeq \mathrm{Additive}\,\mathbb{Z}^\times, hence cardinality 2 and nontriviality. Under this equivalence H^0 of gmPowSelf p becomes u \mapsto u^p on \mathbb{Z}^\times, so its kernel corresponds to the kernel of powMonoidHom p; together with the identification of H^0(\mu_p) with that kernel this gives the bijection muPH0EquivIntUnitsPthTorsion H^0(\mu_p) \simeq \ker(u \mapsto u^p \text{ on } \mathbb{Z}^\times) (a bijection of types, with the underlying unit described explicitly via H^0 of the inclusion \mu_p \to \mathbb{G}_m), and the count \#H^0(\mu_p) = 2 for p = 2 and 1 for p an odd prime. Finally, assuming \#H^1_{\mathrm{fppf}}(\operatorname{Spec}\mathbb{Z}, \mathbb{G}_m) = 1, one obtains \#H^1(\mu_p) = \#H^0(\mu_p) = \#\bigl(\mathbb{Z}^\times/(\mathbb{Z}^\times)^p\bigr), equal to 2 for p = 2 and to 1 otherwise. Triviality of H^1(\mathbb{G}_m) is carried throughout as a named hypothesis.

Relation to Mathlib

The cohomology groups are Mathlib's sheaf cohomology Sheaf.H (Ext from the constant \mathbb{Z}-sheaf), the fppf topology on schemes is Mathlib's Scheme.fppfTopology, and \operatorname{Spec}\mathbb{Z} is terminal by Mathlib's specZIsTerminal; the long-exact-sequence wrappers, the representable \mathbb{G}_m-sheaf and its universe-lifted form, and the Kummer sequence are the project's own constructions.

Where it is used

These computations form the value layer of the fppf Kummer calculus over \operatorname{Spec}\mathbb{Z}, supplying orders of H^0 and H^1 of \mu_p to the interface used on the modular-curve side of the argument.

theorem cohomologyδ_injective_of_subsingleton {S : ShortComplex (Sheaf J AddCommGrpCat.{w})}
    (hS : S.ShortExact) (n₀ n₁ : ℕ) (h : n₀ + 1 = n₁)
    (hvanish : Subsingleton (S.X₂.H n₀)) :
    Function.Injective (cohomologyδ hS n₀ n₁ h) := by
  refine (injective_iff_map_eq_zero _).mpr fun x hx => ?_
  obtain ⟨y, rfl⟩ := (cohomology_exact_three hS n₀ n₁ h x).mp hx
  rw [Subsingleton.elim y 0, map_zero]

theorem cohomologyδ_surjective_of_subsingleton {S : ShortComplex (Sheaf J AddCommGrpCat.{w})}
    (hS : S.ShortExact) (n₀ n₁ : ℕ) (h : n₀ + 1 = n₁)
    (hvanish : Subsingleton (S.X₂.H n₁)) :
    Function.Surjective (cohomologyδ hS n₀ n₁ h) := fun y =>
  (cohomology_exact_one hS n₀ n₁ h y).mp (Subsingleton.elim _ _)

theorem cohomologyδ_bijective_of_subsingleton {S : ShortComplex (Sheaf J AddCommGrpCat.{w})}

    Function.Bijective (cohomologyδ hS n₀ n₁ h) :=
