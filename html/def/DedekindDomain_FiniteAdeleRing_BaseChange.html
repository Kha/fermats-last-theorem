Finite adele rings: base change along an AKLB extension

The module is organised in three layers. First, ContinuousSemialgHom φ A B (notation A →SA[φ] B) is a semialgebra homomorphism along a ring map \varphi : R \to S — additive, multiplicative, unital, and satisfying \psi(r \cdot a) = \varphi(r) \cdot \psi(a) — together with a continuity field; the accompanying class ContinuousSemialgHomClass, the FunLike and coercion instances, ContinuousSemialgHom.commutes (\psi \circ \mathrm{algebraMap} = \mathrm{algebraMap} \circ \varphi), and the products prod, prodMap complete the bundled-morphism API. Second, for a family of rings R_i with subring-like subobjects B_i and modules M_i with subgroups C_i stable under B_i, the restricted product \prod'_i (R_i,B_i) acts componentwise on \prod'_i (M_i,C_i), making the latter a module over the former; a linear map between such restricted products is shown to be determined by its components M_i \to M_{2,i} (linearMap_component, obtained by evaluating at single), from which openness criteria (isOpenMap_linearMap_of_surjective), the identification \prod'_i (n \to R_i, \ \prod_n B_i) \cong n \to \prod'_i(R_i,B_i) as a continuous linear equivalence, and the statement that a finitely generated restricted-product module carries the module topology (RestrictedProduct.isModuleTopology) are derived. The classes Pi.FiberwiseSMul, Prod.IsProdSMul and RestrictedProduct.FiberwiseSMul record that a given scalar action on a product, a binary product, or a restricted product is computed coordinatewise along a map of index sets. Third, in the AKLB situation (A a Dedekind domain with fraction field K, B a Dedekind domain finite over A with fraction field L, compatible towers), mapRingHom and mapSemialgHom assemble the local maps K_v \to L_w for w above v into a continuous semialgebra homomorphism \mathbb{A}^f_K \to \mathbb{A}^f_L along K \to L. Under a fixed \mathbb{A}^f_K-algebra structure on \mathbb{A}^f_L whose action is fibrewise (ComapFiberwiseSMul) and with L/K finite, a chain of linear equivalences L \otimes_K \mathbb{A}^f_K \cong B \otimes_A \mathbb{A}^f_K \cong \prod'_v (B \otimes_A K_v) \cong \prod'_v \prod_{w \mid v} L_w \cong \mathbb{A}^f_L yields bijectivity of the base-changed map and hence the algebra isomorphisms baseChangeAlgEquiv (over L) and baseChangeAdeleAlgEquiv (over \mathbb{A}^f_K); \mathbb{A}^f_L is then a finite \mathbb{A}^f_K-module carrying the module topology, and the isomorphisms are upgraded to continuous algebra isomorphisms baseChangeAdeleContinuousAlgEquiv and baseChangeContinuousAlgEquiv.

Relation to Mathlib

Mathlib supplies SemialgHom, the restricted product Πʳ and IsDedekindDomain.FiniteAdeleRing; the continuous variant ContinuousSemialgHom with its morphism class, the module structure of a restricted product of modules over a restricted product of rings, and the adelic base-change equivalences are the project's own additions.

Where it is used

The identification L \otimes_K \mathbb{A}^f_K \cong \mathbb{A}^f_L, as a topological algebra isomorphism over both L and \mathbb{A}^f_K, is what allows adelic automorphic objects over a base field to be compared with those over a finite extension, as needed when automorphic forms on quaternion algebras over totally real fields are set up in the modularity-lifting part of the argument.
