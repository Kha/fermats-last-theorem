A third bidegree-(2,2) addition law on Weierstrass projective models

Let R be a commutative ring and W a Weierstrass curve over R, with projective defining polynomial W.toProjective.polynomial in R[X_0,X_1,X_2]. Working in R[\,X_{\mathrm{inl}\,0},X_{\mathrm{inl}\,1},X_{\mathrm{inl}\,2},X_{\mathrm{inr}\,0},X_{\mathrm{inr}\,1},X_{\mathrm{inr}\,2}] — the coordinate ring of a pair of points, the two copies of the curve equation being WP_1 and WP_2, the images of the projective polynomial under the two renamings — the module writes down three explicit polynomials kw_lrThird_X, kw_lrThird_Y, kw_lrThird_Z. Each is a sum of terms C(c)\cdot m_{\mathrm l}\cdot m_{\mathrm r} in which m_{\mathrm l} and m_{\mathrm r} are monomials of degree 2 in the left and in the right triple of variables respectively, and c is an explicit polynomial in a_1,a_2,a_3,a_4,a_6; so the triple is bihomogeneous of bidegree (2,2), and the three accompanying lemmas record homogeneity of total degree 4.

Two further groups of results relate this triple to the data of the imported module. First, kw_lrThird_pXZ, kw_lrThird_qXZ, kw_lrThird_pYZ, kw_lrThird_qYZ are explicit cofactors, and kw_lrThird_minor_XZ and kw_lrThird_minor_YZ are the polynomial identities T_XA_Z-T_ZA_X=p_{XZ}WP_1+q_{XZ}WP_2,\qquad T_YA_Z-T_ZA_Y=p_{YZ}WP_1+q_{YZ}WP_2, where (A_X,A_Y,A_Z)=(kw_lrAdd_X, kw_lrAdd_Y, kw_lrAdd_Z) is the chord triple of the imported module; the companion statements kw_lrThird_minor_XZ_mem_span and kw_lrThird_minor_YZ_mem_span restate these as membership of the two 2\times2 minors in the ideal generated by WP_1 and WP_2, i.e. proportionality of the two triples modulo the pair of curve equations. Second, kw_lrThird_X_diag, kw_lrThird_Y_diag, kw_lrThird_Z_diag state that substituting the same triple of variables for both the left and the right variables (the algebra map \delta given by aeval (Sum.elim X X)) carries T_X,T_Y,T_Z to the doubling polynomials kw_lrSym_dblX, kw_lrSym_dblY, kw_lrSym_dblZ — so unlike the chord and symmetric laws this triple restricts on the diagonal to the doubling triple itself rather than to a multiple of it.

Relation to Mathlib

Mathlib supplies the Weierstrass curve structure and its projective defining polynomial (WeierstrassCurve.toProjective, WeierstrassCurve.Projective.polynomial) together with the homogeneity API for multivariate polynomials; the bidegree-(2,2) addition-law triples, their cofactors and the minor identities are the project's own.

Where it is used

Together with the chord triple and the symmetric triple of the imported module, this triple provides an addition law on the projective Weierstrass model that is defined along the diagonal, so that addition and doubling are given by universal polynomial formulae over an arbitrary base ring.
