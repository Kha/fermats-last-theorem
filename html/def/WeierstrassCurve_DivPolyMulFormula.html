Reduced division polynomials: multiplication formula predicates

Fix a Weierstrass curve W and work with Mathlib's reduced division polynomials \mathrm{pre\Psi}, \Psi^2 = \mathrm{\Psi Sq}, \Phi, together with \Psi_2^2, \Psi_3, \mathrm{pre\Psi}_4. Three predicates are introduced. For a nonsingular affine point (x,y) on W over a field F and m : \mathbb{N}, MFred m h asserts the conjunction \Psi^2_m(x) \ne 0 and x(mP)\,\Psi^2_m(x) = \Phi_m(x), where P = (x,y) and the left-hand factor is xOrZero of m \bullet P, i.e. the x-coordinate of the point, taken to be 0 at the point at infinity. LowTorsionFree m h asserts that kP \ne 0 for every natural k with 1 \le k \le m; it is monotone in m. For W over a commutative ring R and j : \mathbb{Z}, StarIdentity j asserts the polynomial identity, evaluated at every t \in R, 2t\,\Phi_j(\Phi_j + t\Psi^2_j) + b_2 t\,\Phi_j\Psi^2_j + b_4(\Phi_j + t\Psi^2_j)\Psi^2_j + b_6 (\Psi^2_j)^2 = \Phi_{j+1}\Psi^2_{j-1} + \Phi_{j-1}\Psi^2_{j+1}, all terms evaluated at t.

The remaining declarations supply the algebra around these notions: parity-resolved forms of \Psi^2_m, \Phi_m and of the recursions for \mathrm{pre\Psi} at 2m\pm1, 2m+2; the consecutive-index identities (X\Psi^2_m - \Phi_m)^2 = \Psi^2_{m+1}\Psi^2_{m-1}, \Phi_{m+1}\Psi^2_m - \Phi_m\Psi^2_{m+1} = -\mathrm{pre\Psi}_{2m+1} and \Phi_{m+1}\Psi^2_{m-1} - \Phi_{m-1}\Psi^2_{m+1} = -\mathrm{pre\Psi}_{2m}\Psi_2^2; closed forms at small index (\mathrm{pre\Psi}'_5, \mathrm{pre\Psi}'_6, \mathrm{pre\Psi}'_7, \Psi^2_5, \Psi^2_7, \Phi_5); the instances ellipticNet_one to ellipticNet_four of the above identity for j \le 4, each a long explicit expansion in b_2,b_4,b_6,b_8 modulo the relation 4b_8 = b_2b_6 - b_4^2; doubling and addition formulae relating \mathrm{addX} to \Psi_2^2 and \Phi_2; the induction producing MFred from LowTorsionFree together with the star identities; and the consequences linking vanishing of \Psi^2_{m}(x) to mP = 0.

Relation to Mathlib

The division polynomials preΨ, preΨ', ΨSq, Φ, Ψ₂Sq, Ψ₃, preΨ₄ and the relation b_relation are Mathlib's; xOrZero is the project's x-coordinate function on affine points, extended by 0 at infinity. The predicates MFred, LowTorsionFree and StarIdentity are the project's own, as are several variants of Mathlib-style lemmas on addX and slope restated for W.toAffine.

Where it is used

These identities and predicates underlie the comparison between the x-coordinate of mP and the reduced division polynomials, and hence between torsion points and roots of \Psi^2_m; they feed the bounds on p-torsion of an elliptic curve and the Tate-curve x-coordinate alignment used in the net-pairing track.

def MFred (m : ℕ) {x y : F} (h : W.toAffine.Nonsingular x y) : Prop :=
  (W.ΨSq (m : ℤ)).eval x ≠ 0 ∧
    (m • (Point.some x y h : W.toAffine.Point)).xOrZero * (W.ΨSq (m : ℤ)).eval x =
      (W.Φ (m : ℤ)).eval x

def LowTorsionFree (m : ℕ) {x y : F} (h : W.toAffine.Nonsingular x y) : Prop :=
  ∀ k : ℕ, 1 ≤ k → k ≤ m → k • (Point.some x y h : W.toAffine.Point) ≠ 0

lemma LowTorsionFree.mono {m n : ℕ} (hmn : m ≤ n) {x y : F} {h : W.toAffine.Nonsingular x y}
    (hf : LowTorsionFree n h) : LowTorsionFree m h :=
  fun k hk1 hkm => hf k hk1 (hkm.trans hmn)

end Statement
