Tate's abstract residue and cotrace–residue identities

Throughout, a Place of an extension F/K is a valuation subring of F containing the image of K, proper in F and a principal ideal ring, hence a discrete valuation ring, with associated order function ord, residue field and uniformizer.

The first group of definitions sets up Tate's construction over a base field K. For a K-linear endomorphism \varphi of a K-vector space whose image is finite dimensional, finrankTrace is the trace of \varphi restricted to \operatorname{range}\varphi. For three endomorphisms, tateComm pA φ ψ is the composite p_A\varphi p_A\psi - p_A\psi p_A\varphi (in diagrammatic order as written); its values lie in \operatorname{range} p_A, it is antisymmetric in \varphi,\psi, and tateCommRestrict is the induced endomorphism of \operatorname{range} p_A, whose finrankTrace is tateCommTrace. When \varphi\psi=\psi\varphi, tateComm_eq_of_commute rewrites the commutator as p_A\psi(\varphi a-p_A\varphi a)-p_A\varphi(\psi a-p_A\psi a). For a place u of L/K, adicIntegersKSubmod is the ring of integers of the completion \hat L_u viewed as a K-submodule, tateProj is the projection of \hat L_u onto it along a chosen complement, lmulK is multiplication by an element of \hat L_u as a K-endomorphism, and tateRes u fh gh is tateCommTrace of the two multiplication operators against this projector, defined when the relevant range is finite dimensional.

Four Prop-valued predicates record the expected properties: KwF4gRRTateCommFinite (that finiteness always holds), KwF4gRRTateAgreement (tateRes against a uniformizer equals the K-trace from the residue field of kwHgfV352_localResidueCompletion, the local residue of an integral approximant of an element of the completion), KwF4gRRTateChainRule (replacing a uniformizer pulled back from E by the uniformizer at w at the cost of multiplying by the differential coefficient of the pullback of v's \mathrm{d}-coordinate), and KwF4gRRTateTraceCompat (compatibility with kwHgfV352_completionTraceAt, the local trace of g\in F at w\mid v read in \hat E_v).

The remaining predicates concern Kähler differentials: kaehlerPullback and kaehlerCotrace are KaehlerDifferential.map K K E F applied to an element, resp. as an E-linear map \Omega_{E/K}\to\Omega_{F/K}; Place.regularSubmodule w ωF is the K-submodule of f\in F with f\cdot w.\mathrm{differentialCoeff}\,\omega_F in the valuation ring at w. KwHgfV352CompletionTraceSum asserts that the image of \mathrm{Tr}_{F/E}g in \hat E_v is the sum of the local completion traces over the fibre of v; KwHgfV352R3MPGKPowBasisLocal asserts the corresponding residue-term identity for the elements \theta^j(\pi^{k+1})^{-1} of a local power basis, and KwF4R1V391aResidueTraceCompletionCommute the same identity for arbitrary g\in F with \omega_E=v.\mathrm{dCoord}. Finally FiberKaehlerCotraceResidueIdentityK asserts that, given canonical local residue data at each place of F and a nonzero \omega_E with nonzero pullback, there is a family of such data at the places of E for which the sum of residue terms over the fibre of v equals the residue term of \omega_E at the trace \mathrm{Tr}_{F/E}f; CotraceResidueIdentityOnFiberLocalizedK is the variant restricted to those p\in F failing to lie in regularSubmodule at at most one place of the fibre.

Relation to Mathlib

The trace, the module of Kähler differentials and the functoriality map KaehlerDifferential.map are Mathlib's; Place, the places' completions and all residue notions, as well as Tate's commutator residue, are the project's own.

Where it is used

These definitions organise the proof of the residue theorem for function fields along Tate's route, which underlies the Riemann–Roch theory used for modular curves and their Jacobians in the Frey curve argument.

section TateComm

variable {K V : Type*} [Field K] [AddCommGroup V] [Module K V]

def tateComm (pA φ ψ : V →ₗ[K] V) : V →ₗ[K] V :=
  pA ∘ₗ φ ∘ₗ pA ∘ₗ ψ - pA ∘ₗ ψ ∘ₗ pA ∘ₗ φ

theorem tateComm_apply (pA φ ψ : V →ₗ[K] V) (x : V) :
    tateComm pA φ ψ x = pA (φ (pA (ψ x))) - pA (ψ (pA (φ x))) := rfl

theorem tateComm_mem_range (pA φ ψ : V →ₗ[K] V) (x : V) :
    tateComm pA φ ψ x ∈ LinearMap.range pA :=
  sub_mem (LinearMap.mem_range_self pA _) (LinearMap.mem_range_self pA _)

theorem tateComm_antisymm (pA φ ψ : V →ₗ[K] V) :
    tateComm pA φ ψ = - tateComm pA ψ φ := by
  unfold tateComm; rw [neg_sub]

def tateCommRestrict (pA φ ψ : V →ₗ[K] V) :
    LinearMap.range pA →ₗ[K] LinearMap.range pA :=
  (tateComm pA φ ψ).restrict (fun x _ => tateComm_mem_range pA φ ψ x)

theorem tateCommRestrict_apply (pA φ ψ : V →ₗ[K] V) (a : LinearMap.range pA) :
    (tateCommRestrict pA φ ψ a : V) = tateComm pA φ ψ (a : V) := rfl

def tateCommTrace (pA φ ψ : V →ₗ[K] V)
    [FiniteDimensional K (LinearMap.range (tateCommRestrict pA φ ψ))] : K :=
  finrankTrace (tateCommRestrict pA φ ψ)

end TateComm
