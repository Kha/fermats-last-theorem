definition module

Per-node conclusion frame for descended semistable nodes

This module defines a single Prop-valued predicate, ModularCurve.FullLevel.PernodeConclusion, which packages the local assertion made at one node of the descended model. Its arguments are a prime q, a level M'\ne 0, a valuation subring A of \overline{\mathbb Q}, a finite set W of places of \mathrm{modularFunctionFieldC}(\kappa_A,M') over the residue field \kappa_A of A, a ConstantReduction R_0 from the base-changed level-M' function field to that field, a chosen s\in W, a subfield k_0\subseteq\overline{\mathbb Q} with an element \pi_0 lying in A, a prime \ell', a subfield K_\ell of the Laurent series field over k_0 with a nonzero element j_\ell, the hypothesis that the level-q\ell' j-expansion jqNModC k₀ (q * ℓ') lies in K_\ell, a constant a_0\in A_0:=A\cap k_0, a subfield K_0\subseteq K_\ell over k_0, a subalgebra B_0\subseteq K_\ell, a valuation subring W_0' of K_0, a finite set nodes of subrings of K_0 and one subring \mathcal O, together with further ambient data of the descent situation (\ell,\zeta_0,\varpi_t,K_b,A_b,\varpi_b,\xi, an ideal y, a subalgebra B_t, a valuation subring W_t, integers n,m, \gamma_0\in \mathrm{SL}_2(\mathbb Z), a k_0-automorphism \tau_0 of K_\ell, an element \zeta_c of the completion of A_0, a finite set ends and a subgroup G), summarised here; the body constrains the first group.

The predicate asserts: \mathcal O\subseteq W_0'; \mathcal O is local and Noetherian, meets k_0 exactly in A_0, and every element of \mathcal O is congruent modulo non-units to a constant from A_0 (so its residue field is that of A_0). Next, there is E_0\ge 1 with \pi_0^{E_0}=\varpi_t^{2w}v for a unit v of A_0, where w=\mathrm{placeWidthChar}\,q\,M'\,s, together with c_x,c_y\in\mathcal O, a unit u, and a ring isomorphism \iota of the adic completion \hat{\mathcal O} with the crossing model \hat A_0[[U,V]]/(UV-\pi_0^{E_0}) carrying constants to constants, such that c_xc_y=\pi_0^{E_0}u and \iota(c_x),\iota(c_y) are unit multiples of U and V; c_y lies in the maximal ideal of W_0' and c_x, when it lies in W_0', is a unit there. Further, j_{q\ell'}-a_0 lies in K_0 and in \mathcal O, with \iota of it a unit times V^e for some e\ge 1. There is a valuation subring W_x\supseteq\mathcal O of K_0 meeting k_0 in A_0, a discrete valuation ring with maximal ideal generated by \pi_0, containing an element whose reduction satisfies no relation with A-integral coefficients, with c_x a non-unit and c_y a unit; on W_x the level-M' dictionary holds: any g in the full level-M' function field which is R_0-integral and has no pole where j_q has none has its \ell'-expansion in K_0 and in W_x, lying in the maximal ideal exactly when R_0.\mathrm{residue}(g) vanishes, and, when that residue is finite at s, lying in \mathcal O with residue the value s(R_0.\mathrm{residue}(g)); moreover every other \mathcal O'\in nodes contains an element outside W_x. Finally there is a subring B_x\subseteq\mathcal O mapping into B_0, regular wherever j_\ell is, with \mathcal O its localisation at the elements of B_x that are units in \mathcal O, with fraction field K_0, generated over the constants by a finite set, and containing an element b having a pole at every valuation subring of K_0 which has constants A_0, residually kills \pi_0, receives j residually transcendentally, and fails to contain \mathcal O.

Relation to Mathlib

The predicate is assembled from Mathlib's ValuationSubring, AdicCompletion, IsDiscreteValuationRing, Ideal.span and IsLocalRing.maximalIdeal; the crossing model \hat A_0[[U,V]]/(UV-\pi) (UVCrossingModel, a quotient of Mathlib's MvPowerSeries in two variables), the reductions with prescribed constant field (ConstantReduction), the modular function fields and the width function placeWidthChar are the project's own, Mathlib having no notion of a node of a semistable model of a modular curve.

Where it is used

The predicate is the block asserted at each node by the descent theorem that produces, over the constants A_0, a semistable local picture of the full-level modular curve at q: each node has local ring with completed crossing model \hat A_0[[U,V]]/(UV-\pi_0^{E_0}), one branch being the Igusa branch on which the level-M' functions specialise to their values at the supersingular place s, the other recorded by W_0', with j_{q\ell'}-a_0 vanishing only along the latter. Packaging it as a definition lets the statements about the descended nodes quote the whole local picture in one token.
