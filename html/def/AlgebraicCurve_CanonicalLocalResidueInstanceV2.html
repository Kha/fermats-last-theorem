Local residues at places: pole filtration, canonical data, sections

For a field extension F/K and a place v of F/K (a valuation subring with principal maximal ideal containing K), the module builds residue maps and the structures they require. A distinguished irreducible element uniformizerSubring of \mathcal O_v lifting v.uniformizer is fixed, and the pole filtration is defined: poleSubmodule n is the K-submodule \{f\in F : \pi^n f\in\mathcal O_v\}, equal to \mathcal O_v for n=0, to simplePoleSubmodule for n=1, characterised by -n\le\operatorname{ord}_v f for f\neq0, monotone in n with supremum F; laurentTailCoeff n is the K-linear map sending f to the residue class of \pi^n f in \kappa(v). On the simple-pole submodule the residue f\mapsto \overline{\pi f} is defined outright; localResidueExtend is a chosen K-linear extension of it to F, which yields a LocalResidueData and hence the unconditional instance HasLocalResidue K F. Subtracting higherPoleCorrection — a linear map vanishing on poleSubmodule 1 and matching the extension on the classes of \pi^{-(m+2)}, which are linearly independent modulo poleSubmodule 1 — produces a CanonicalLocalResidueDataK, i.e. a residue vanishing on \mathcal O_v, equal to \overline{\pi f} on simple poles and zero on \pi^{-(n+1)} for n\ge 1; this gives the instance HasCanonicalLocalResidueK K F. Two such data agree on all pole submodules when K\to\kappa(v) is surjective. For larger residue fields, CoefficientFieldSection is a K-algebra map \kappa(v)\to\mathcal O_v splitting the residue map, CanonicalLocalResidueDataS \sigma adds vanishing on \sigma(a)\pi^{-(n+1)}, n\ge 1, and such data again agree on pole submodules. A parallel development replaces \mathcal O_v by its adic completion \widehat{\mathcal O}_v: the level-one evaluation lg37_residueHat onto \kappa(v) is surjective with kernel the maximal ideal, the completion is Henselian and local, separable monic polynomials lift roots from \kappa(v) uniquely, Lg37CompletionSection records a K-algebra section of lg37_residueHat, one such section is obtained from a primitive element of \kappa(v)/K together with a lifted root of its minimal polynomial, and every element has a unique truncated Taylor expansion \sum_{i<n}\sigma(a_i)\pi^i modulo \mathfrak m^n. Auxiliary instances provide the residue-field algebra and tower structure for the restriction of a place along F\subseteq F', and the nonvanishing of \operatorname{Tr}_{\kappa(v)/K} (HasSeparableResidue) when K is perfect and all residue fields are finite over K.

Relation to Mathlib

The residue data structures, the pole filtration, the coefficient-field and completion sections are the project's own notions; they are built on Mathlib's AdicCompletion, HenselianLocalRing/HenselianRing, IsLocalRing.ResidueField and discrete valuation ring machinery. Mathlib has no Cohen coefficient-field theorem, which is why sections are introduced as data here.

Where it is used

These constructions discharge the HasLocalResidue, HasCanonicalLocalResidueK and HasSeparableResidue hypotheses carried by the project's residue-theorem and Riemann–Roch development for function fields of curves, which in turn underlies the treatment of modular curves in the modularity part of the argument.

private theorem ord_nonneg_of_mem {f : F} (hf : f ∈ v.toValuationSubring) : 0 ≤ v.ord f := by
  rcases eq_or_ne f 0 with rfl | hf0
  · simp
  obtain ⟨π, hπ⟩ := IsDiscreteValuationRing.exists_irreducible v.toValuationSubring
  obtain ⟨n, u, hu⟩ :=
    IsDiscreteValuationRing.eq_unit_mul_pow_irreducible
      (x := (⟨f, hf⟩ : v.toValuationSubring)) (by simpa [Subtype.ext_iff] using hf0) hπ
  have hcoe : f = ((u : v.toValuationSubring) : F) * ((π : F) ^ (n : ℤ)) := by
    have h := congrArg (Subtype.val) hu
    push_cast at h
    rw [zpow_natCast]
    exact h
  rw [hcoe, v.ord_unit_smul_zpow u hπ (n : ℤ)]
  exact Int.natCast_nonneg n

private theorem mem_of_ord_nonneg {f : F} (hf : f ≠ 0) (h : 0 ≤ v.ord f) :
    f ∈ v.toValuationSubring := by
  obtain ⟨π, hπ⟩ := IsDiscreteValuationRing.exists_irreducible v.toValuationSubring
  obtain ⟨u, hu⟩ := v.exists_unit_mul_zpow hf hπ
  rw [hu, show v.ord f = (((v.ord f).toNat : ℕ) : ℤ) from (Int.toNat_of_nonneg h).symm,
    zpow_natCast]
  exact mul_mem (u : v.toValuationSubring).2 (pow_mem (π : v.toValuationSubring).2 _)

  smul_mem' c f hf := by
