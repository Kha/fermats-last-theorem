Site layer for the resolved model at level N0​q

Standing context: a prime q not dividing N_0, a Deligne–Rapoport model package \mathfrak X of level (N_0,q), a discrete valuation domain O with a ring map \rho_O from the package's base ring, and an algebraically closed field \kappa of characteristic q. Here DRLevel.XO ρO is the base change X of the model to \operatorname{Spec} O, and Node 𝔛 κ τ is the point set of the fibre product of the two component maps 𝔛.comp κ τ 0, 𝔛.comp κ τ 1 into the geometric special fibre; xpt sends a node n to its image x_n in X (first projection, then comp 0, then a base-change map bc).

The predicates are chart hypotheses on this X. HasOrientedCrossingCharts asserts: for each node n there are e\ge 1, an open U\ni x_n and a morphism f\colon U\to\operatorname{Spec}\bigl(O[X_0,X_1]/(X_0X_1-q^e)\bigr) over \operatorname{Spec} O (i.e. f followed by the structure map equals U.\iota followed by the projection of X to \operatorname{Spec} O) such that the two distinguished elements CrossingQuotient.U, CrossingQuotient.V both lie in the prime at f(y) exactly when y\mapsto x_n; at points over x_n the stalk map of f is flat, carries the maximal ideal onto the maximal ideal and induces an isomorphism of residue fields; and, in both directions, V lies in the prime at f(y) iff y is in the image of comp 0 ≫ bc, and U iff y is in the image of comp 1 ≫ bc. ModelHyps q O is a Prop-structure carrying, for the toric resolutions \rho_e\colon \text{Resolution} \to crossing scheme: that \rho_e restricted over the union of the basic opens of U and V is an isomorphism (e>0), that \rho_e is proper, that \rho_e followed by the map to \operatorname{Spec} O is flat, that the resolution is locally Noetherian, and that the complement of the preimage of that union has empty interior. EtNbhd carries, as a hypothesis, the local criterion: a morphism locally of finite presentation which at a point is flat, with maximal ideal mapping onto maximal ideal and residue field map an isomorphism, is étale on some open neighbourhood.

ChartData bundles the data and the seven conditions for one node, and chartData extracts it from HasOrientedCrossingCharts. rawChartInputOfDR then builds a RawChartInput on X indexed by the nodes: distinguished points x_n (assumed distinct and closed), target crossing schemes, the open V_c given by the two basic opens, the resolutions and their maps, thickness e, charts U, f (shown locally of finite presentation), base \operatorname{Spec} O, and the properness, flatness, Noetherian and interior clauses from ModelHyps; simp lemmas identify its U_0, f_0 and thickness with the chart data. With finitely many nodes and EtNbhd, chartInputOfDR upgrades this to a ChartInput.

In the second namespace the base-change map bc = DRLevel.bcMap ρO toκ is fixed, with bc_fst, bc_snd recording its commutation with the two projections, and SiteHyps collects node finiteness, oriented charts, EtNbhd, injectivity and closedness of the x_n, and ModelHyps. Assuming moreover that the maximal ideal of O is generated by q, one obtains the chart and glue inputs R, C, D, the glued scheme Y with its maps toDR to X and toBase to \operatorname{Spec} O, the description of the open locus X_0 as the complement of the x_n, the widths width n = e_n, an isomorphism chartIso between each glue chart and the preimage toDR^{-1}(U_n), the two points \xi_0,\xi_1 obtained by pushing the generic point of the geometric special fibre through the two component maps, the identification vertex_eq of the chart vertex with Resolution.vertexPt, the resolution origins oRes and line generic points gRes over that vertex (with hoRes, hgRes), and finally the component ideal sheaves comp, their generic points η and the edge points edgePt on Y, together with chainPos'_eq matching the glue's chain position function with DRResolvedModelPackageLevel.chainPos. An auxiliary lemma states that a morphism locally of finite type into a locally Noetherian scheme is locally of finite presentation.

Relation to Mathlib

The chart, glue and crossing-resolution notions are the project's own; Mathlib supplies the ambient scheme theory used (properness, flatness, IsLocallyNoetherian, Scheme.IdealSheafData, ideal-sheaf supports). The auxiliary lemma transfers Mathlib's Algebra.FinitePresentation.of_finiteType from rings to schemes, and EtNbhd carries as a hypothesis a local criterion for étaleness not available in Mathlib in this form.

Where it is used

These data and hypotheses are what a producer uses to fill a ModularCurve.DRResolvedModelPackageLevel: a regular model over O of the modular curve of level N_0q whose special fibre consists of two copies of the level-N_0 fibre joined at the crossing points by chains of rational curves. That resolved model underlies the component-group and Jacobian computations used for level lowering at q.

def EtNbhd : Prop :=
  ∀ {U S : Scheme.{0}} (f : U ⟶ S) [LocallyOfFinitePresentation f] (x : U),
    (f.stalkMap x).hom.Flat →
    Ideal.map (f.stalkMap x).hom (IsLocalRing.maximalIdeal (S.presheaf.stalk (f.base x))) =
      IsLocalRing.maximalIdeal (U.presheaf.stalk x) →
    ∀ [IsIso (f.residueFieldMap x)], ∃ V : U.Opens, x ∈ V ∧ Etale (V.ι ≫ f)
