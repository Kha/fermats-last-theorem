Module topologies: topological modules, scalar change, permanence properties

This module collects additions to the theory of the module topology. Two classes are introduced. First, IsTopologicalModule R M, for a ring R with a topology and a topological R-module M, which simply bundles ContinuousSMul R M together with ContinuousAdd M; it is shown to be inherited along an inducing R-linear map (Topology.IsInducing.topologicalModule), and instances are provided for submodules with the subspace topology and for products \prod_i M_i with the product topology. Second, IsBiscalar R S f, a class on a map f : A \to B between additive monoids each carrying both an R- and an S-module structure, asserting the two equations f(r \cdot a) = r \cdot f(a) and f(s \cdot a) = s \cdot f(a); from it, LinearMap.changeScalars, LinearEquiv.changeScalars, AlgHom.changeScalars and AlgEquiv.changeScalars re-read an S-linear (resp. S-algebra) map as an S'-linear (resp. S'-algebra) map with the same underlying function, and IsBiscalar.commutes records that such an f commutes with the structure maps from S'. Separately, AlgEquiv.extendScalars turns an A-algebra equivalence f : C \simeq_A D, where C is an algebra over an intermediate A-algebra B, into a B-algebra equivalence, D being given the B-algebra structure transported along f restricted to B.

The remaining declarations concern IsModuleTopology, the predicate that the given topology on M coincides with the module topology moduleTopology R M; ModuleTopology.isModuleTopology and ModuleTopology.iff record the tautological instance and this characterisation. For a commutative topological ring R and a module-topologised finite R-algebra D: multiplication is continuous and D is a topological semiring, resp. topological ring (continuous_mul', continuous_mul, topologicalSemiring, Module.topologicalRing), the underlying tool being continuity of an R-bilinear map on a finite free first factor. Further results: the module topology makes M a topological module; for an R-algebra S which is a topological ring, S is a topological R-module exactly when \mathrm{algebraMap} is continuous; comparison \mathrm{moduleTopology}\,R\,M \le \mathrm{moduleTopology}\,S\,M and, when S is a module-topologised finite R-algebra, equality of the two module topologies on an S-module M with compatible R-action, hence IsModuleTopology R M ↔ IsModuleTopology S M; a criterion via a continuous open algebraMap; descent along linear quotient maps, open surjections and split continuous maps; automatic continuity of linear and algebra equivalences between module-topologised objects, including IsBiscalar variants producing A \simeq_L B and A \simeq_A B; Hausdorffness of free modules over a Hausdorff base with continuous addition and multiplication (and the field case); local compactness of finite modules over a locally compact topological ring; the homeomorphic coordinate isomorphism R \simeq_L (\mathrm{Fin}\,(\dim_K R) \to K) for a finite-dimensional K-algebra; and second countability of finite modules over a second countable base.

Relation to Mathlib

Mathlib supplies moduleTopology and IsModuleTopology; this module adds further permanence and descent lemmas for them. The classes IsTopologicalModule (a bundling of ContinuousSMul and ContinuousAdd, which Mathlib keeps separate) and IsBiscalar, together with the changeScalars constructions, are introduced here.

Where it is used

The module topology is the standard way to topologise finite algebras and their base changes — completions of number fields, finite-dimensional algebras over local fields and over the adeles, and tensor products M \otimes_R A — so that they are automatically topological rings, Hausdorff, locally compact and second countable. These permanence properties are what make such objects usable as topological groups in the automorphic side of the argument.

lemma _root_.Algebra.moduleTopology_le [IsTopologicalModule R S] :
    moduleTopology R M ≤ moduleTopology S M := by
  letI : TopologicalSpace M := moduleTopology S M
  haveI : ContinuousAdd M := continuousAdd S M
  have ⟨cts_smul⟩ : ContinuousSMul S M := continuousSMul S M
  suffices ContinuousSMul R M from _root_.moduleTopology_le R M
  constructor
  suffices Continuous (fun rm ↦ algebraMap R S rm.1 • rm.2 : R × M → M) by
    simpa [← algebra_compatible_smul S]
  fun_prop

lemma _root_.moduleTopology.trans [IsTopologicalRing R] [Module.Finite R S] [IsModuleTopology R S] :
    moduleTopology R M = moduleTopology S M := by
  have := IsModuleTopology.isTopologicalModule
  refine le_antisymm (Algebra.moduleTopology_le _ _ _) ?_
  letI : TopologicalSpace M := moduleTopology R M
  haveI : IsModuleTopology R M := isModuleTopology R M
  haveI : ContinuousAdd M := continuousAdd R M
  have ⟨cts_smul⟩ : ContinuousSMul R M := continuousSMul R M
  suffices ContinuousSMul S M from _root_.moduleTopology_le S M
  constructor
  let bil : S →ₗ[R] M →ₗ[R] M := {
    toFun s := {
      toFun m := s • m
      map_add' := DistribSMul.smul_add s
      map_smul' := smul_comm s
    }
    map_add' s t := by
      ext m
      exact Module.add_smul s t m
    map_smul' r s := by
      ext m
      exact IsScalarTower.smul_assoc r s m
  }
  exact continuous_bilinear_of_finite_left bil
