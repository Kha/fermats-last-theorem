Abstract double-coset Hecke operators on fixed points

Fix a group G acting on an additive commutative monoid A by a distributive action, subgroups U, V \le G and an element g \in G. The module first equips Mathlib's set of fixed points fixedPoints V A with algebraic structure: it is an additive commutative monoid (as the fixed-point additive submonoid), and for a scalar type R acting on A commutingly with G it carries the induced scalar multiplication, giving a MulAction and, when R is a ring acting on A by a module structure, a Module structure; coe_zero, coe_add, coe_smul record that the coercion to A preserves these operations. The main definition is the double-coset operator. Under the hypothesis h that the image of the set U \cdot \{g\} = Ug under the projection G \to G/V is finite — equivalently, that UgV is a finite union of left cosets g_i V — HeckeOperator_toFun sends a \in A^V to \sum_i g_i \cdot a, where the g_i are the canonical Quotient.out representatives of the cosets in the image of Ug in G/V and the sum is an unconditional finsum over that set. Two points are worth noting: the representatives are not a chosen decomposition but the canonical section of the quotient, and the proof that the value lies in A^U proceeds by the auxiliary results Set.bijOn_smul (left translation by u \in U is a bijection of U \cdot X onto itself) and eq_finsum_quotient_out_of_bijOn' (for a fixed by V, the sum of g \cdot a over any set of representatives bijecting onto a subset of G/V agrees with the sum over the canonical representatives). The operator is then packaged as an additive homomorphism HeckeOperator_addMonoidHom and, for R a ring acting commutingly, as an R-linear map HeckeOperator : fixedPoints V A →ₗ[R] fixedPoints U A; HeckeOperator_apply states its defining formula on underlying elements.

Relation to Mathlib

Mathlib supplies the fixed-point set MulAction.fixedPoints and its additive submonoid; the additive monoid, scalar, MulAction and Module instances on it are added here. Mathlib has no general double-coset Hecke operator, so HeckeOperator is the project's own.

Where it is used

This is the general double-coset formalism [UgV] \colon A^V \to A^U from which the Hecke action on spaces of automorphic forms is obtained: taking G to be the adelic points of a totally definite quaternion algebra and U, V open compact subgroups gives the Brandt-matrix action on weight-two quaternionic automorphic forms, and in the \mathrm{GL}_2 setting the classical operators T_p and U_p.

lemma eq_finsum_quotient_out_of_bijOn' (a : fixedPoints V A)
    {X : Set (G ⧸ V)}
    {s : Set G} (hs : s.BijOn (QuotientGroup.mk : G → G ⧸ V) X) :
    ∑ᶠ g ∈ s, g • (a : A) = ∑ᶠ g ∈ Quotient.out '' X, g • (a : A) := by
  let e (g : G) : G := Quotient.out (QuotientGroup.mk g : G ⧸ V)
  have he₀ : Set.BijOn e s (Quotient.out '' X) := by
    refine Set.BijOn.comp ?_ hs
    exact Set.InjOn.bijOn_image <| Set.injOn_of_injective Quotient.out_injective
  have he₁ : ∀ g ∈ s, g • (a : A) = (Quotient.out (QuotientGroup.mk g : G ⧸ V)) • a := by
    intro g hgs
    obtain ⟨v, hv⟩ := QuotientGroup.mk_out_eq_mul V g
    rw [hv, mul_smul, (show (v : G) • (a : A) = a from a.2 v)]
  exact finsum_mem_eq_of_bijOn e he₀ he₁

noncomputable def HeckeOperator_toFun (a : fixedPoints V A) : fixedPoints U A :=
  ⟨∑ᶠ gᵢ ∈ Quotient.out '' (QuotientGroup.mk '' (U * {g}) : Set (G ⧸ V)), gᵢ • a.1, by
  rintro ⟨u, huU⟩
  rw [smul_finsum_mem (h.image Quotient.out), ← eq_finsum_quotient_out_of_bijOn' a]
  · rw [finsum_mem_eq_of_bijOn (fun g ↦ u • g)]
    · exact Set.InjOn.bijOn_image <| Set.injOn_of_injective (MulAction.injective u)
    · simp [mul_smul]
  · apply (Set.bijOn_comp_iff (Set.injOn_of_injective (MulAction.injective u))).1
    change Set.BijOn ((fun xbar ↦ u • xbar) ∘ (QuotientGroup.mk : G → G ⧸ V)) _ _
    rw [Set.bijOn_comp_iff]
    · rw [← Set.image_comp]
      simp only [Function.comp_apply, Quotient.out_eq, Set.image_id']
      refine Set.bijOn_image_image (f := fun (x : G) ↦ u • x) (p₁ := (QuotientGroup.mk : G → G ⧸ V))
        (fun a ↦ rfl) ?_ (Set.injOn_of_injective (MulAction.injective u))
      apply Set.bijOn_smul _ _ _ huU
    · refine Set.InjOn.image_of_comp ?_
      simp only [Function.comp_def, Quotient.out_eq]
      exact Function.Injective.injOn Function.injective_id
    ⟩
