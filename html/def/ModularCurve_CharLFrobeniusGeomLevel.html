Characteristic-ℓ Frobenius and Hecke operators on modular function fields

Fix a field K of characteristic \ell (\ell prime), an integer N \neq 0, a datum data : ModularPolynomialData ℓ and a hypothesis hKr : KroneckerCongruence ℓ data. Inside K((q)) the field modularFunctionFieldC K N is the intermediate field generated over K by jqModC K (the Laurent series q^{-1} times the reduction of the integral power series jNum, i.e. the q-expansion of j) and by jqNModC K N, its image under the substitution q \mapsto q^N. The substitution q \mapsto q^\ell, packaged as a K-algebra endomorphism qExpandAlgC K ℓ of K((q)), raises each of these two generators to the \ell-th power, by the identity j(q^\ell) = j(q)^\ell recorded in ModularPolynomialData.frobenius_identity_geom; hence it maps the field into itself, and frobeniusGeomLevel is the resulting injective K-algebra endomorphism, with frobeniusGeomLevelImage its image and frobeniusGeomLevelEquiv the isomorphism onto that image. Every \ell-th power lies in the image, so the extension is purely inseparable of exponent dividing \ell: isFrobeniusEndo_frobeniusGeomLevel verifies both clauses of IsFrobeniusEndo ℓ, namely that each x^\ell is a value and each value is an \ell-th power, given that K is closed under \ell-th roots.

On places, frobOnPlacesGeomLevel sends w to the place whose valuation subring consists of the x with frobeniusGeomLevel x in that of w; it is injective, and surjective under the same perfectness hypothesis, via IsFrobeniusEndo.extendPlace, which pulls a place back along the ring homomorphism frobNormRingHom given by the inverse-image map frobNorm. verOnPlacesGeomLevel is a classically chosen one-sided inverse. Ramification is computed: \mathrm{ord}_w(\mathrm{Frob}\,g) = \ell\,\mathrm{ord}_{\mathrm{Frob}(w)}(g).

On divisors, frobeniusPushforwardGeomLevel transports supports along frobOnPlacesGeomLevel, frobeniusPullbackGeomLevel sends \langle v, n\rangle to \langle \mathrm{Ver}(v), n\ell\rangle, and heckeFibreGeomLevel is their sum. Their composite satisfies \mathrm{Frob}^*\mathrm{Frob}_* = \ell, degrees are multiplied by 1, \ell and \ell+1 when all places have degree one, and all three preserve principality; they therefore descend to degree-zero divisors and to Pic0, where eichlerShimura_special_fibre_pic0_level gives \mathrm{Frob}_*^2 - T\,\mathrm{Frob}_* + \ell = 0. The …OfIsCurveOver variants supply the perfectness and degree-one hypotheses from K algebraically closed together with IsCurveOver K (modularFunctionFieldC K N).

Relation to Mathlib

The framework of places, divisors, degree-zero divisors and Pic0 for a function field, the predicate IsCurveOver, and IsFrobeniusEndo are the project's own; Mathlib supplies the ambient LaurentSeries, IntermediateField, ValuationSubring and Finsupp material. Some general lemmas on valuation subrings and on ramification indices of restricted places are proved here alongside.

Where it is used

These operators model the Frobenius and Verschiebung morphisms, and the Hecke correspondence T_\ell, on the characteristic-\ell fibre of the modular curve of level N, the Eichler–Shimura congruence relation on its Jacobian being what links Hecke eigenvalues to traces of Frobenius in the Galois representations attached to modular forms.

    (A : ValuationSubring F) {x : F} {n : ℕ} (hn : n ≠ 0) (h : x ^ n ∈ A) : x ∈ A := by
  rcases A.mem_or_inv_mem x with hx | hx
  · exact hx
  rcases eq_or_ne x 0 with rfl | hx0
  · exact A.zero_mem
  have hpow : x ^ n = x * x ^ (n - 1) := by

    rw [hpow, inv_pow, mul_assoc, mul_inv_cancel₀ (pow_ne_zero _ hx0), mul_one]
  rw [← hkey]
