Hopf ideals and quotient Hopf algebras

Let R be a commutative ring and H a commutative Hopf R-algebra, with comultiplication \Delta, counit \varepsilon and antipode S, and let \pi\colon H\to H/I denote the quotient map attached to an ideal I\subseteq H. The class Ideal.IsHopfIdeal' R I is the predicate with three fields: (\pi\otimes\pi)(\Delta x)=0 in (H/I)\otimes_R(H/I) for all x\in I, \varepsilon(x)=0 for all x\in I, and S(x)\in I for all x\in I. The first field is the kernel form of the usual condition \Delta(I)\subseteq I\otimes_R H+H\otimes_R I, the image of the latter being the kernel of \pi\otimes\pi. The zero ideal is registered as a Hopf ideal. For a Hopf ideal I, the maps HopfAlgebra.HopfIdealQuotient.comul, counit and antipode are the R-algebra maps H/I\to (H/I)\otimes_R(H/I), H/I\to R, H/I\to H/I obtained from (\pi\otimes\pi)\circ\Delta, \varepsilon and \pi\circ S by factoring through I; coassociativity, the two counit identities and the two antipode identities are proved for them, yielding Bialgebra R (H ⧸ I) and HopfAlgebra R (H ⧸ I), so that \Delta_{H/I}(\pi x)=(\pi\otimes\pi)(\Delta x), \varepsilon_{H/I}(\pi x)=\varepsilon(x) and S_{H/I}(\pi x)=\pi(Sx). Cocommutativity of H passes to H/I. On the map side: quotientBialgHom is \pi as a bialgebra homomorphism, quotientFactorBialgHom the bialgebra map H/I\to H/J for I\le J (both surjective, and compatible under composition); quotientLiftPoint and quotientPointsEquiv give the bijection between R-algebra maps H/I\to A and R-algebra maps H\to A vanishing on I, for A a commutative R-algebra. Finite type and, for I=\bot (where H/\bot\cong H as R-algebras), flatness are inherited. A preliminary section treats the antipode as an algebra endomorphism of a commutative Hopf algebra and shows that for \varphi\colon H\to A the elements \varphi and \varphi\circ S are mutually inverse for the convolution product, so every such \varphi is a unit in the convolution monoid; convCompMonoidHom records that precomposition with a bialgebra map H\to G is a monoid homomorphism for convolution.

Relation to Mathlib

Mathlib supplies Bialgebra, HopfAlgebra, BialgHom and the convolution monoid WithConv on algebra homomorphisms; the Hopf-ideal predicate Ideal.IsHopfIdeal' and the Hopf-algebra structure on the quotient by a Hopf ideal are added here.

Where it is used

Quotients of commutative Hopf algebras by Hopf ideals correspond to closed subgroup schemes of affine group schemes, and the point-lifting bijection describes the functor of points of such a quotient; this interface underlies the project's treatment of affine group schemes.

section IsHopfIdeal'

variable (R : Type*) [CommRing R] {H : Type*} [CommRing H] [HopfAlgebra R H]

class Ideal.IsHopfIdeal' (I : Ideal H) : Prop where
  map_mkₐ_comul_eq_zero : ∀ x ∈ I,
    Algebra.TensorProduct.map (Ideal.Quotient.mkₐ R I) (Ideal.Quotient.mkₐ R I)
      (Coalgebra.comul (R := R) x) = 0
  counit_eq_zero : ∀ x ∈ I, Coalgebra.counit (R := R) x = 0
  antipode_mem : ∀ x ∈ I, HopfAlgebra.antipode R x ∈ I

instance Ideal.isHopfIdeal_bot : (⊥ : Ideal H).IsHopfIdeal' R where
  map_mkₐ_comul_eq_zero x hx := by rw [(Ideal.mem_bot).1 hx]; simp
  counit_eq_zero x hx := by rw [(Ideal.mem_bot).1 hx]; simp
  antipode_mem x hx := by rw [(Ideal.mem_bot).1 hx]; simp

end IsHopfIdeal'

def quotientLiftPoint (I : Ideal H) (f : H →ₐ[R] A) (hf : ∀ x ∈ I, f x = 0) : H ⧸ I →ₐ[R] A :=
  Ideal.Quotient.liftₐ I f hf

    (quotientLiftPoint R I f hf).comp (Ideal.Quotient.mkₐ R I) = f :=
  Ideal.Quotient.liftₐ_comp _ _ _

theorem comp_mkₐ_apply_eq_zero (I : Ideal H) (g : H ⧸ I →ₐ[R] A) (x : H) (hx : x ∈ I) :
    g.comp (Ideal.Quotient.mkₐ R I) x = 0 := by

  fun _ _ h => Ideal.Quotient.algHom_ext R h

    quotientLiftPoint R I (g.comp (Ideal.Quotient.mkₐ R I))

  comp_mkₐ_injective R I (quotientLiftPoint_comp_mkₐ R I _ _)

def quotientPointsEquiv (I : Ideal H) :
