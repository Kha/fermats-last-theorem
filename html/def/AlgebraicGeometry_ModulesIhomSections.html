Sections of the internal Hom of sheaves of modules

The module equips the closed symmetric monoidal structure on presheaves and sheaves of modules with an explicit description on sections. At the presheaf level, for presheaves of modules F,N over R\colon C^{\mathrm{op}}\to\mathbf{CommRing}, the sections of ihomObj F N over X are by construction the natural families \varphi: families of R(Y)-linear maps \varphi_f\colon F(Y)\to N(Y) indexed by f\colon X\to Y with N(g)(\varphi_f(x))=\varphi_{f\circ g}(F(g)(x)). The auxiliary namespace IsSheafAux identifies this presheaf, as a presheaf of types, with the subfunctor linearSub of Mathlib's presheafHom cut out by R-linearity of the components over the over-categories (toPresheafHom, ofPresheafHom, equivLinearSub, isoLinearSub), and linearSub_local shows linearity is a local condition when N is separated; PresheafOfModules.ihomObj_isSheaf concludes that if the underlying presheaf of N is a J-sheaf, so is that of ihomObj F N.

Over a ringed site (\mathcal O,h\mathcal O) this gives ihomModel, the presheaf internal Hom of the underlying presheaves, which is a sheaf, so the unit ihomModelToIhom into the sheafified internal Hom is an isomorphism; ihomSectionsEquivFamily is the resulting additive identification of \Gamma(X,(\mathrm{ihom}\,M)N) with natural families, and ihomEval evaluates a section t at a section m over the same object by applying the component at the identity. Its additivity, \mathcal O-linearity in both variables and compatibility with restriction are recorded, together with tmulSection, the elementary tensor of two sections of M and P inside (M\otimes P), its bilinearity and restriction rules, and the comparison isomorphisms sheafifyTensorIso, sheafifyTensorNatIso, unitIhomNatIso through which ihom_adjunction_eq identifies the sheaf-level tensor–internal-Hom adjunction with the composite of the presheaf adjunction and sheafification restricted along the fully faithful inclusion. Consequently curry, uncurry, ihom.ev, MonoidalClosed.pre, functoriality in N, the left unitor and MonoidalClosed.unitIsoSelf are computed on sections in terms of ihomEval and tmulSection, with extensionality principles for sections of the internal Hom and for morphisms out of a tensor product. A general lemma CategoryTheory.MonoidalClosed.unitIsoSelf_hom_eq expresses the forward map of unitIsoSelf in any monoidal closed category as \lambda^{-1} followed by evaluation at the unit.

For a scheme X the same data are phrased geometrically: restrictHomAppLE turns a morphism M|_U\to N|_U into \Gamma(X,V)-linear maps \Gamma(M,V)\to\Gamma(N,V) for V\le U, restrictHomEquivFamily is the additive bijection between morphisms M|_U\to N|_U and natural families over U, and composing it with the previous identification yields ihomSectionsEquiv, \Gamma((\mathrm{ihom}\,M)N,U)\cong\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathcal O_U}(M|_U,N|_U), and ihomGlobalSectionsEquiv over U=\top, \Gamma((\mathrm{ihom}\,M)N,\top)\cong\operatorname{Hom}(M,N), both compatible with restriction and with ihomEval. Finally tmulSection_eq_tensorSections matches the tensor of sections with the one defined earlier, and restrictUnitIso' is the isomorphism (\mathbb 1_{Y\text{-Mod}})|_f\cong\mathbb 1_{X\text{-Mod}} for an open immersion f\colon X\to Y, built from the isomorphism \mathcal O_Y(f(U))\cong\mathcal O_X(U).

Relation to Mathlib

Mathlib supplies presheaves and sheaves of modules, the sheafification adjunction, presheafHom, Subfunctor and the localisation of monoidal structures; the internal-Hom presheaf of natural families, its sheaf property, and the description of sections of the internal Hom of \mathcal O_X-modules as morphisms between restrictions are the project's own. CategoryTheory.MonoidalClosed.unitIsoSelf_hom_eq is a general lemma about arbitrary monoidal closed categories added here.

Where it is used

This is general foundational material on \mathcal O_X-modules: it provides the section-level calculus (evaluation, elementary tensors, curry/uncurry, duals) for the closed symmetric monoidal category of sheaves of modules on a scheme, which many later modules of the development use when working with invertible sheaves and their duals.

lemma linearSub_local {J : GrothendieckTopology C}
    (hNsep : Presieve.IsSeparated J (N.presheaf ⋙ forget AddCommGrpCat))
    (X : Cᵒᵖ) (s : (presheafHom F.presheaf N.presheaf).obj X)
    (hs : (linearSub F N).sieveOfSection s ∈ J X.unop) : s ∈ (linearSub F N).obj X := by
  intro Y r x
  obtain ⟨Y⟩ := Y
  apply (hNsep _ (J.pullback_stable Y.hom hs)).ext
  intro W k hk

  have nat : ∀ x' : F.obj (Opposite.op Y.left),
      appAt s (Opposite.op (Over.mk (k ≫ Y.hom))) (F.map k.op x') =
        N.map k.op (appAt s (Opposite.op Y) x') := fun x' =>
    ConcreteCategory.congr_hom (s.naturality (Over.homMk k rfl : Over.mk (k ≫ Y.hom) ⟶ Y).op) x'

  have hlin : ∀ (ρ : R.obj (Opposite.op W)) (z : F.obj (Opposite.op W)),
      appAt s (Opposite.op (Over.mk (k ≫ Y.hom))) (ρ • z) =
        ρ • appAt s (Opposite.op (Over.mk (k ≫ Y.hom))) z := fun ρ z => by
    have h := hk (Opposite.op (Over.mk (𝟙 W))) ρ z
    have e := presheafHom_map_app_op_mk_id (F := F.presheaf) (G := N.presheaf) (k ≫ Y.hom) s
    dsimp only [appAt] at h ⊢
    erw [e] at h
    exact h
  change N.map k.op (appAt s (Opposite.op Y) (r • x)) = N.map k.op (r • appAt s (Opposite.op Y) x)
  rw [← nat, N.map_smul, ← nat, F.map_smul]
  exact hlin _ _
