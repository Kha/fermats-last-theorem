definition module

Crossing charts and glue data for resolved DR models

Fix a prime p, a package \mathfrak X of Deligne–Rapoport model data for level p, a discrete valuation domain O and an algebraically closed field \kappa of characteristic p. Here X_O is the base change pullback (DRModel.toBase p) (Spec.map (algebraMap ℤ O)) of the model to \operatorname{Spec} O, the index type Node is the set of points of the fibre product of the two component maps \mathfrak X.\mathrm{compInf}\,\kappa and \mathfrak X.\mathrm{compZero}\,\kappa, and xpt sends a node to its image in X_O along a given base-change morphism bc. Three predicates record the geometric input, each kept as a hypothesis. HasOrientedCrossingCharts asserts that every node n admits e\ge 1, an open U\ni \mathrm{xpt}(n) and a morphism f\colon U\to crossingScheme ((p:O)^e), the spectrum of O[X_0,X_1]/(X_0X_1-p^e), compatible over \operatorname{Spec} O with the projection of U, such that both distinguished elements U and V lie in the prime f(y) exactly when y\mapsto \mathrm{xpt}(n); at points over \mathrm{xpt}(n) the stalk map is flat, carries the maximal ideal onto the maximal ideal and induces an isomorphism of residue fields; and the chart is oriented, in that V\in f(y) holds precisely for y in the image of the \infty-component and U\in f(y) precisely for y in the image of the 0-component. ModelHyps bundles five properties of the toric resolutions \rho_e: \rho_e restricts to an isomorphism over D(U)\cup D(V) for e>0, is proper, the composite to \operatorname{Spec} O is flat, each resolution is locally Noetherian, and the preimage of the vertex locus has empty interior. EtNbhd asserts the spreading-out statement that a morphism locally of finite presentation which at a point is flat, carries maximal ideal to maximal ideal and induces a residue-field isomorphism is étale on some open neighbourhood of that point.

From these, ChartData packages one chosen chart per node, chartData extracts it, and rawChartInputOfDR, chartInputOfDR assemble a RawChartInput, respectively ChartInput, on X_O indexed by the nodes, with S_n the crossing scheme, V_c the union of the two basic opens, thickness e_n, resolution \rho_{e_n}, and base \operatorname{Spec} O; finite presentation of the charts comes from a lemma proved here, that a morphism locally of finite type into a locally Noetherian scheme is locally of finite presentation. The V3Asm section fixes a ring map \mathrm{to}\kappa\colon O\to\kappa, takes bc to be the induced base-change morphism (with its compatibilities with both projections), and bundles the above into a single hypothesis structure SiteHyps. Under these and the hypothesis that the maximal ideal of O is generated by p, it defines the glued scheme Y with its morphisms to X_O and to \operatorname{Spec} O, the chart isomorphisms onto the preimages of the chart opens, the widths e_n, the two points \xi_0,\xi_1 given by the generic points of the two components of the special fibre, the resolution points oRes (origins) and gRes (generic points of the exceptional lines) together with proofs that both lie over the vertex, the vertex identification vertex_eq with the explicit vertex prime, and finally the ideal-sheaf components comp, their generic points η and the edge points edgePt, plus the identification of the glue's chain position with the chain position used by DRResolvedModelPackage.

Relation to Mathlib

The morphism classes and scheme-theoretic notions used (LocallyOfFiniteType, LocallyOfFinitePresentation, Etale, Flat, IsProper, IsLocallyNoetherian, Scheme.IdealSheafData) are Mathlib's; the crossing scheme, its toric resolutions, the chart and glue inputs, and the model packages are the project's own notions.

Where it is used

These definitions form the site layer producing a regular resolved model over O of the Deligne–Rapoport model of X_0(p), whose special fibre consists of the two components meeting in chains of rational curves at the supersingular points; the resulting DRResolvedModelPackage is the geometric input for the analysis of the Jacobian's Néron model and component group used on the Frey–Serre–Ribet side of the argument.

def EtNbhd : Prop :=
  ∀ {U S : Scheme.{0}} (f : U ⟶ S) [LocallyOfFinitePresentation f] (x : U),
    (f.stalkMap x).hom.Flat →
    Ideal.map (f.stalkMap x).hom (IsLocalRing.maximalIdeal (S.presheaf.stalk (f.base x))) =
      IsLocalRing.maximalIdeal (U.presheaf.stalk x) →
    ∀ [IsIso (f.residueFieldMap x)], ∃ V : U.Opens, x ∈ V ∧ Etale (V.ι ≫ f)
